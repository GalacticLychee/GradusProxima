# Paper Information
Author; Simon Watkins
Date; August 3rd, 1970
Organization; ESA

# Abstract
UK citizens have a neutral to positive opinion of space exploration.

# Paper
This study was conducted across 250 UK citizens in London by selecting people riding the bus using systematic sampling. The subjects were asked to rate their approval of space exploration from 1 to 5, and this resulted in an average score of 3.7 with a standard deviation of 1.4.
