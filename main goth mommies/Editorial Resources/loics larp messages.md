# 1967.
During Mariner 5, they had attached a small UV light detector to produce false images of Venus in uses for propaganda. When Mariner 5 reached Venus, they had noticed something strange with the stars... One of them was moving, and it wasn't Mercury or any other planet for that matter.

NASA was completely baffled as to what this could be, or why they have never seen it. After looking through previous databanks and local libraries, they found an extremely likely though extremely surprising explanation - The Planet Vulcan. A completely new object found right next to us. Why did we not confirm this earlier?

We already had with the discovery by Le Verrier, though was shut down multiple times as sunspots, mercury, and more recently Einsteins theory of relativity (though never denying a smaller tiny moon sized planet existing within the mercury orbit). Even Emmanuel Liais, who did not believe in the existence of the planet in 1859 had also seen it, until tragedy struck, as a fire occurred in a house with both Le Verrier and Lescarbault both in it, ultimately killing both and erasing some evidence. Since then, Vulcan had been forgotten and when other amateur astronomers had reportedly seen it, they denied it with other reasons.

And in 1908, William Wallace Campbell, Director, and Charles Dillon Perrine, Astronomer, of the Lick Observatory, after comprehensive photographic observations at two solar eclipse expeditions in 1905, and 1908, stated: "In our opinion, the work of the two Crocker Expeditions ... brings the observational side of the intermercurial planet problem, for half a century, definitely to a close." Nobody had tried to purposefully look for it after that except for some who still believed in the planet.

That brings us to today, with more advanced technology and a satellite capable of sending images back to earth near Venus, denied all of this and a small planet around the Sun did exist. Months later, NASA released proof as the public went wild.
